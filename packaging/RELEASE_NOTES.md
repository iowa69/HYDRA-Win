Portable Windows build of [Hydra](https://github.com/iowa69/hydra) (vendored at commit `75ecbe8`) by Giovanni Lorenzin · IOWA-BioTech.

**Install:** unzip into a folder you can write to (Downloads or Desktop, not Program Files), then double-click `HYDRA-Win.exe`. Thirteen of the fourteen gene and typing databases, the organism typing data for all eight organisms, the PubMLST MLST schemes and a Mash sketch of 60 clinical species are in the zip, so **species identification and MLST work with no network at all**. On first launch HYDRA-Win downloads only what a licence does not let it carry: CARD, the Ridom *spa* database, hicap's *cap* database and ShigEiFinder's gene sets. If this PC cannot reach them, import a copy from another PC (**Databases… → Import from folder…** or `--import-db`).

**What's new in 0.7.0**

- **AMR genes are called by AMRFinderPlus's own rules, and measured against AMRFinderPlus itself.** HYDRA-Win can't run NCBI's `amrfinder`, which is built for Linux and macOS only; it searches AMRFinderPlus's database with upstream Hydra's BLAST code. It now applies AMRFinderPlus's rules, read from that database and from NCBI's source code, instead of one 90% identity for every gene:
  - **NCBI's curated identity cut-off for each gene family** (826 families: 98% for the *aac(3)* and *dfrA* families, 85% for *cfr*), moving up to the parent family when a hit misses its own family's, and 90% where NCBI sets none.
  - **An allele name only for an exact match.** Anything less is named by its family, with the closest allele next to it: `blaKPC (closest blaKPC-2)`. One amino acid can be another allele with another phenotype (KPC-33 is KPC-2 plus D179Y). Internal family IDs such as `fosA5_fam` and `mecI_of_mecA` no longer appear as gene names.
  - A TTG or GTG start codon reads as methionine, so those genes are exact matches again. A premature stop codon makes the call `INTERNAL_STOP`, which is never counted. A fusion gene carries both its names (`aac(6')-Ie/aph(2'')-Ia`). A gene family NCBI marks as not reportable is not reported.
  - **Measured on 48 RefSeq genomes of 16 species** (one complete genome and two drafts each), with AMRFinderPlus 4.2.7 and the same database release: **all 392 of AMRFinderPlus's AMR gene calls are found at the same place under the same name** (336 in 0.6.0), and 114 of 117 point mutations agree. Every call is in `docs/amrfinder_comparison.md`; `tools/compare_amrfinder.py` reruns it.
- **The Summary counts what AMRFinderPlus reports.** A gene is counted only where the AMRFinderPlus protein search calls it too. A confident call that only MEGARes, ARG-ANNOT, ResFinder or CARD makes (mostly chromosomal efflux pumps, regulators and housekeeping genes NCBI leaves out of AMRFinderPlus on purpose, such as *acrA*, *cpxAR*, *mexQ*) is listed under **AMR genes in other catalogues only (not counted)**. A typical *E. coli* showed 54-60 AMR genes; it now shows 3-11 (AMRFinderPlus: 3-10). Counted by gene name, the Summary's AMR genes are AMRFinderPlus's in 44 of the 48 genomes. Drug classes follow the counted genes. **Expect lower AMR gene counts than 0.6.0 for most genomes.**
- **Fixed: a gene split by an insertion was reported as complete, and could hide the gene inside it.** Two pieces of a *blaZ* 5.5 kb apart were joined into one complete, exact *blaZ*, and the *aac(6')-Ie/aph(2'')-Ia* gene between them (high-level gentamicin resistance) was filed as a duplicate of it and left off the Summary. Pieces more than 60 bp apart are no longer joined; the larger is reported as partial, which is what AMRFinderPlus reports.
- **Fixed: a gene with a premature stop codon was counted.** The protein search now sees the stop, and a nucleotide match from another database, which cannot see it, no longer confirms the gene.
- Still different from AMRFinderPlus, and listed in the README: no HMM search, no mosaic-PBP calls (upstream Hydra does not import the database file they come from; the PBP point mutations are reported), and contig-end partials reported as partial.

**What was new in 0.6.0**

- **Sixty more species are identified from the whole genome, not from their MLST scheme.** Kleborate's sketch, which upstream Hydra uses, covers the Enterobacterales only, so every other genome used to be named from its MLST scheme — and several schemes are shared by neighbours. HYDRA-Win now ships its own Mash sketch of 498 NCBI RefSeq genomes of 60 clinical species: the staphylococci, enterococci and streptococci, *Listeria*, *C. difficile*, *Pseudomonas*, *Acinetobacter*, *Stenotrophomonas*, the *B. cepacia* complex, *Haemophilus*, *Neisseria*, *Campylobacter*, *Vibrio*, *Aeromonas* and others. Every genome passed NCBI's own ANI taxonomy check; `tools/clinical_species.tsv` lists the species and why each is there, and why the *M. tuberculosis* complex, the *B. cereus* group, *Brucella* and *B. mallei* are deliberately not (a Mash distance cannot separate them from a neighbour).
  - **Measured on 292 draft assemblies the sketch had never seen: 291 named correctly, none wrongly** (284 of 285 leaving out 7 identical to a sketch genome at its resolution). One *S. oralis* was too far from every sketch genome and was named from its MLST scheme, as before. The closest pairs (*N. gonorrhoeae* / *N. meningitidis* / *N. lactamica*, *S. pneumoniae* / *S. pseudopneumoniae*) were separated by at least 0.02 every time. Every genome and result is in `docs/species_sketch_validation.md`.
  - What it changes, checked end to end through the pipeline with MLST: an *S. argenteus* (ST2250) is no longer reported as *S. aureus* with the *S. aureus* catalogue and typing; an *A. pittii* is no longer reported as *A. baumannii*; a gonococcus is no longer "*Neisseria* sp." with no mutation catalogue — it gets AMRFinderPlus's gonococcal one (*penA*, *mtrR*, *gyrA* S91F) and not the meningococcal typing module; every *B. cepacia* complex member gets the complex's catalogue.
- **Only confident calls are counted.** The Summary sheet, the window and `Cumulative_results.xlsx` count and list an AMR gene only when one database at its locus matches it at **≥ 90% identity over ≥ 90% of the gene, complete** (not cut by a contig end, no internal stop codon). The bar is fixed, whatever the detection thresholds are set to. Everything else is listed under **AMR genes to check**, with the reason (`blaTEM-1 (partial: 72.4% of the reference gene)`), and every hit stays on its sheet with a new **Confident call** column. Drug classes now include established point mutations, so `gyrA` S83I puts quinolones on the Summary with no *qnr* gene present.
- **Fixed:** `HYDRA-Win.exe --import-db FOLDER`, `--import-db=FOLDER` and `--db-status=FILE` opened the window instead of running headless. The `--import-db` help no longer says it installs PubMLST, and two README passages that still said PubMLST and the meningococcal data are downloaded are corrected. A database whose source states no version no longer reads `unknown` in the workbook: it says so in words, with the pinned abricate commit and the retrieval date.

**Databases and licences.** Which data ships in the zip follows the project owner's policy: a source that states no licence or terms is shipped; a source that expressly restricts redistribution is downloaded on your own PC. Two sets are downloaded although their licence would permit redistribution — hicap's *cap* database and ShigEiFinder's gene sets are GPL-3.0, which is copyleft, and this zip is MIT. The Ridom *spa* database is expressly limited to non-commercial use by people who download it themselves. CARD withholds redistribution without McMaster University's written consent. PubMLST is shipped because its terms expressly permit redistributing the pre-2025 data an anonymous download returns, and that is all the snapshot contains; commercial users should note that PubMLST requires a separate licence for data submitted from 2025 onwards, none of which is in this zip or reachable from it.

**Input:** FASTA assemblies (`.fasta`, `.fa`, `.fna`, `.fas`, optionally `.gz`). Reads (FASTQ) aren't analysed.

**Output:** one Excel workbook per genome (Summary, AMR_genes, Point_mutations, Virulence, Plasmids, Typing, All_hits, Pivot), plus an optional `Cumulative_results.xlsx` (Summary, AMR_matrix, Mutations_matrix, Virulence_matrix, Plasmids_matrix, Typing_matrix, All_hits).

**Headless:** `HYDRA-Win.exe --cli <fasta...> --out DIR [--cumulative] [--min-identity PCT] [--min-coverage PCT] [--threads N]`; `--import-db FOLDER` installs the downloaded databases from a copy; `--db-status` prints what is installed.

**Bundled databases:** NCBI AMRFinderPlus, ResFinder, PlasmidFinder, SCCmecFinder, Kleborate v3.2.4 typing loci and species sketches, HYDRA-Win's clinical species sketch (NCBI RefSeq), VFDB (core), ARG-ANNOT, MEGARes, E. coli VF, EcOH, the PubMLST 7-locus MLST schemes (pre-2025 snapshot), and the organism typing data for *S. aureus* (except spa), *S. pyogenes*, *S. agalactiae*, *S. pneumoniae*, *H. influenzae* (except the *cap* locus), *N. meningitidis* (all of it), *Salmonella* and *E. coli*/*Shigella* (except the Shigella/EIEC cluster). NCBI BLAST+ 2.16.0 is included too. See `db/DATABASES.txt` for sources, dates, licences and citations.
