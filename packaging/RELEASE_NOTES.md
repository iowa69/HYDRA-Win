Portable Windows build of [Hydra](https://github.com/iowa69/hydra) (vendored at commit `75ecbe8`) by Giovanni Lorenzin · IOWA-BioTech.

**Install:** unzip into a folder you can write to (Downloads or Desktop, not Program Files), then double-click `HYDRA-Win.exe`. Thirteen of the fourteen gene and typing databases, and the organism typing data for all eight organisms, are in the zip — including, new in this release, the PubMLST MLST schemes, so **MLST works with no network at all**. On first launch HYDRA-Win downloads only what a licence does not let it carry: CARD, the Ridom *spa* database, hicap's *cap* database and ShigEiFinder's gene sets. If this PC cannot reach them, import a copy from another PC (**Databases… → Import from folder…** or `--import-db`).

**What's new in 0.5.0**

- **MLST works offline.** The PubMLST 7-locus schemes are in the zip, and with them every call derived from a sequence type: the *S. aureus* clonal complex, the *Salmonella* eBurst group, the meningococcal clonal complex and fine type, the *H. influenzae* sequence type, the *E. coli* ST131 subclone. The release test proves it on a fresh unzip with no network: an isolate gets its ST, and `CC8` is derived from it.
  - PubMLST publishes no open licence, and the one sentence this rests on is: *"All data submitted on or before 31 December 2024 may continue to be downloaded, used, and redistributed without restriction, subject only to proper scientific citation and acknowledgement."* The same terms say anonymous users may see only pre-2025 data, so the snapshot is fetched **anonymously, with no API key and no other credential**, and contains only data that sentence covers. The build asks PubMLST's own API to confirm it (an `added_after=2025-01-01` query returns no records, and every allele id in the bundle is checked against the index an anonymous client is served) and fails if the confirmation is missing. `db\mlst\pubmlst\PUBMLST.txt` records the date, the terms and the result.
  - **If you use this data, cite and acknowledge PubMLST:** Jolley KA, Bray JE, Maiden MCJ, *Wellcome Open Res* 2018;3:124, and *"This publication made use of the PubMLST website (https://pubmlst.org/) sited at the University of Oxford. The development of that website was funded by the Wellcome Trust."* Data submitted from 1 January 2025 onwards is not in the zip and is not reachable; an allele defined after that date reads "not determined" with that reason.
- **All the meningococcal typing is in the zip too** — capsule serogroup, PorA/FetA fine type, BAST and the MenDeVAR vaccine-reactivity index, MLST and clonal complex, resistance alleles — because every one of those databases is PubMLST nomenclature. *H. influenzae* now needs only hicap's GPL-3.0 *cap* set downloaded. The allele sets are in the zip **once**: four organism modules read the same schemes, so their own copies are not shipped.
- **A capsule call states the type first and the lesion with it.** A meningococcus carrying a serogroup B locus it cannot express now reads **`B (capsule gene disrupted)`**, not a bare `NG` with the genogroup hidden in another column — both halves are true and belong together, and these lesions revert. A capsule gene cut by a contig end is a third, distinct wording, `B (capsule gene at a contig end, expression not assessed)`. *H. influenzae* uses the same shape: a single-copy *cap* locus with *bexA* truncated reads `b (capsule gene disrupted)`, while the normal virulent Hib arrangement stays plain `b`.
- **Serogroups W, X, Y, E and Z are now validated against genomes with published serogroups** (18 of 18 across the whole set). That validation found a real defect: *csw* (W) and *csy* (Y) are 99% identical, so both cleared any identity threshold, and the tie was being broken by the serogroup's *name* — every serogroup W isolate was reported as Y. The call now goes to whichever serogroup's own gene the isolate matches better, the two scores are in the row's evidence, and a tie too close to call says so and asks for serology.
- **A gene cut by a contig edge is disclosed, not suppressed.** The call stays — a gene running off the end of a contig is a real finding — but nothing in the row lets it pass for a confirmed loss of function: *Type of call* reads "Gene cut by a contig end: NOT evidence of disruption, loss of function not assessed", *Element subtype* is `DISRUPTION_AT_CONTIG_END`, *Resolution* is `CONTIG_END`, the note leads with it, the resistance column says loss of function was not established, and matrix and pivot cells read "cut by contig end - not assessed", struck through. The resistance score ignores such a row.
- **Two calls the reference tools make and HYDRA-Win used to miss**, now reported under the reference tools' own names: `pmrB_A21DfsTer16` (Kleborate's `pmrB:p.Gly20fs`, colistin, *K. pneumoniae* complex) and `acrR_F180LfsTer3` (AMRFinderPlus's own symbol, multidrug efflux, *E. coli*). Checked against ten further public genomes for false positives: every output table was identical with and without them.

**What was new in 0.4.0**

- **Organism-specific typing, applied automatically from the species call.** Eight organisms:
  - ***Staphylococcus aureus*** — spa type, *agr* group, MLST clonal complex, SCCmec, Panton-Valentine leucocidin, *tst*, enterotoxin and haemolysin repertoires, *hlb* status and the Sa3int prophage that interrupts it, capsule serotype locus, immune evasion cluster type, ACME, *blaZ* operon
  - ***Streptococcus pyogenes*** — emm type and cluster with its tissue tropism, virulence and superantigen repertoire, prophage content, regulatory loci, adhesins
  - ***Streptococcus agalactiae*** — capsular serotype, alpha-like protein (Alp) type, surface proteins, pilus island type, marker genes, PBP2X substitutions
  - ***Streptococcus pneumoniae*** — capsular serotype, PBP type (1A:2B:2X) with the CDC reference MIC where that triplet is in the reference table, macrolide/lincosamide/tetracycline/fluoroquinolone determinants, pilus islets
  - ***Haemophilus influenzae*** — capsule type, PBP3 (*ftsI*) substitution pattern and rPBP3 group, β-lactam interpretation, MLST
  - ***Neisseria meningitidis*** — capsule serogroup, fine type, Bexsero antigen sequence type, MLST and clonal complex, resistance target alleles
  - ***Salmonella enterica*** — serovar from the antigenic formula, subspecies, H2 locus, pathogenicity islands, virulence plasmid, typhoidal markers, ST and eBurst group
  - ***Escherichia coli* and *Shigella*** — phylogroup, *fimH* allele, ST131 subclone, Shiga toxin subtype, pathotype, intimin subtype, ExPEC/UPEC panel
- **Every typing call now carries the evidence behind it and its caveats.** The `Typing` sheet is one row per call with the alleles, residues, identity, coverage, contig coordinates and database version the call was read from, and, beside it, what is provisional, what disagreed and what could not be determined. A call that could not be made is coloured and says why; "not determined" is never a negative result.
- **The headline call is in the window's table**, in the `Typing` sheet title and in both Summary sheets: `spa t008 · CC8 · agr I`.
- **New cumulative sheet `Typing_matrix`:** assemblies against typing schemes, the call in every cell, the organism above every column, and the caveat as the cell's comment — a whole batch compared at a glance.
- The existing sheets, the point-mutation detail and the mutation matrices are unchanged.
- `db\DATABASES.txt` now records, for every organism typing database, the exact source, the version or retrieval date, the licence in the source's own words, the redistribution status the source states, and the citation.
- Fixed in the vendored engine: BLAST+ 2.16.0 crashes on a translated search that finds nothing when `-max_target_seqs` is combined with more than one thread. Those searches now run single-threaded, which is what the virulence and toxin panels depend on.

**Databases and licences.** Which data ships in the zip follows the project owner's policy: a source that states no licence or terms is shipped; a source that expressly restricts redistribution is downloaded on your own PC. Two sets are downloaded although their licence would permit redistribution — hicap's *cap* database and ShigEiFinder's gene sets are GPL-3.0, which is copyleft, and this zip is MIT. The Ridom *spa* database is expressly limited to non-commercial use by people who download it themselves. CARD withholds redistribution without McMaster University's written consent. PubMLST is shipped because its terms expressly permit redistributing the pre-2025 data an anonymous download returns, and that is all the snapshot contains; commercial users should note that PubMLST requires a separate licence for data submitted from 2025 onwards, none of which is in this zip or reachable from it.

**Input:** FASTA assemblies (`.fasta`, `.fa`, `.fna`, `.fas`, optionally `.gz`). Reads (FASTQ) aren't analysed.

**Output:** one Excel workbook per genome (Summary, AMR_genes, Point_mutations, Virulence, Plasmids, Typing, All_hits, Pivot), plus an optional `Cumulative_results.xlsx` (Summary, AMR_matrix, Mutations_matrix, Virulence_matrix, Plasmids_matrix, Typing_matrix, All_hits).

**Headless:** `HYDRA-Win.exe --cli <fasta...> --out DIR [--cumulative] [--min-identity PCT] [--min-coverage PCT] [--threads N]`; `--import-db FOLDER` installs the downloaded databases from a copy; `--db-status` prints what is installed.

**Bundled databases:** NCBI AMRFinderPlus, ResFinder, PlasmidFinder, SCCmecFinder, Kleborate v3.2.4 typing loci and species sketches, VFDB (core), ARG-ANNOT, MEGARes, E. coli VF, EcOH, the PubMLST 7-locus MLST schemes (pre-2025 snapshot), and the organism typing data for *S. aureus* (except spa), *S. pyogenes*, *S. agalactiae*, *S. pneumoniae*, *H. influenzae* (except the *cap* locus), *N. meningitidis* (all of it), *Salmonella* and *E. coli*/*Shigella* (except the Shigella/EIEC cluster). NCBI BLAST+ 2.16.0 is included too. See `db/DATABASES.txt` for sources, dates, licences and citations.
